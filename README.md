Expressicord is an unofficial third-party Discord client for Android, built around the Material Expressive 3 design language.

The goal of this project is to deliver a modern, fluid, and Android-first Discord experience with a cleaner interface, smoother interactions, and a stronger focus on Material-inspired visuals.

> [!WARNING]
> Expressicord is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially supported by Discord.
> Using third-party clients may violate Discord's Terms of Service and may result in account restrictions or bans.
> This project was created with AI-assisted / "vibecoded" tooling and is still an experimental work in progress.
> It may contain rough edges, bugs, incomplete features, and unstable behavior.
> Please keep feedback constructive. Rants, rage bait, or comments focused only on "AI"/"vibecoded" drama will not be helpful and will be ignored.
> Use this app at your own risk.

## Why Expressicord?

Discord already works well on mobile, but many users want a more customized, modern, and visually refined Android experience. Expressicord was created to explore that idea by emphasizing:

- A more polished Material-first interface
- Android-focused interaction patterns
- A cleaner and more expressive visual style
- Community-driven experimentation and improvement

## Installation

1. Download the latest APK from the Releases section.
2. On your Android device, enable "Install unknown apps" or "Unknown sources" for your browser or file manager.
3. Open the downloaded APK and follow the installation prompts.
4. Launch Expressicord and sign in with your Discord account.
